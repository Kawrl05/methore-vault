# Alecx — Research Gap

Existing studies on generative AI in game development tend to fall into two buckets:

- Comparisons **between different AI tools/prototypes** rather than between AI and human output ([[Yang Kleinman Harteveld 2024]]).
- Studies on the **ethics of generative AI from the developer's perspective** ([[Boucher et al 2024]]).

What's missing is research on **player perception** — how players actually judge the quality, creativity, and emotional impact of AI-generated content, and whether that judgment tracks with real design quality or with player *beliefs* about the content's origin (see [[Player Perception of AI-Generated Content]]).
