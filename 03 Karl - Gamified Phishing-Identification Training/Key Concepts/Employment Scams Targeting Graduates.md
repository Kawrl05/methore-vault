# Employment Scams Targeting Graduating Students

## Definition
Phishing and fraud schemes that specifically target students entering the job market, exploiting the trust they place in recruitment workflows.

## Key Details
- Reported employment scams **doubled in 2025**; Gen Z applicants were victimized at more than double the rate of Gen X (32% vs. 15%) ([[Zaza 2026]]).
- 40.7% of a surveyed university population encountered suspicious job offers, and 43.2% of those went on to make a payment ([[Anagha et al 2025]]).
- Attackers are now staging **fake technical interviews** that deliver malware through trusted developer tools like GitHub and Visual Studio Code ([[Microsoft Defender 2026]]) — a tactic that specifically targets the workflows CS/IT graduates expect to use.
- This population-specific risk is the direct justification for testing training methods on graduating students rather than a general population (see [[Karl Justification]]).
