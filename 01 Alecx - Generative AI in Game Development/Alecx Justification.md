# Alecx — Justification

The growing use of generative AI in game development raises a tradeoff question: does the efficiency gain come at the cost of content quality, creativity, and player satisfaction?

Two findings motivate the study:

- **Credibility gap.** In [[Velasquez-Salamanca et al 2025]], participants rated AI-generated images lower in credibility (mean 3.527 ± 1.338) than human-made images (mean 4.199 ± 0.944) — see [[Credibility Perception - AI vs Human Images]].
- **Belief-driven perception.** [[Mahsa Cooper 2026]] found players could not reliably tell AI-designed levels from human-designed ones, yet content they *believed* was human-made was rated more fun and aesthetically pleasing, while content believed to be AI-generated was rated more frustrating and less original. This perception bias matters directly for developer trust and credibility — see [[Player Perception of AI-Generated Content]].
