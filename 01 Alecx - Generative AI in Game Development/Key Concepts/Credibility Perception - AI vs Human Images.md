# Credibility Perception: AI vs. Human-Made Images

## Definition
The gap in perceived trustworthiness/credibility between AI-generated and human-made visual content, as rated by viewers.

## Key Details
- [[Velasquez-Salamanca et al 2025]] measured participant credibility ratings (mean ± SD):
  - AI-generated images: **3.527 ± 1.338**
  - Human-created images: **4.199 ± 0.944**
- Human-created images were rated meaningfully more credible.
- This mirrors the belief-driven bias found in games ([[Player Perception of AI-Generated Content]]) — context/labeling of a work as AI-made appears to lower trust regardless of actual quality.
