# Gab — Research Gap

Prior work tends to isolate just one piece of this problem:

- **Risk of AI algorithms to users** on its own.
- **What privacy users give up**, on its own.
- **How transparent platforms are** about data use, on its own.

[[Zakhary et al 2019]] is a close but partial example: they built a system that randomly suggests off-topic content to obfuscate a user's real attributes and reduce profiling/surveillance risk — but it doesn't address platform transparency at all.

No existing paper connects all three threads (risk, tradeoff, transparency) together — and none of them approach the topic through a **gamified evaluation method**, which is the angle this topic proposes.
