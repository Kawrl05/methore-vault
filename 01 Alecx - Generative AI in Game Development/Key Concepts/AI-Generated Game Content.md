# AI-Generated Game Content

## Definition
Game assets — dialogue, art, levels, and other creative material — produced by generative AI models rather than by human designers, typically to speed up development and reduce resource costs.

## Key Details
- Adoption is rising fast: Steam data shows disclosed AI-content usage jumped from ~1% to ~7% of released games year over year ([[Lambe 2025]]).
- The central open question isn't *can AI make content* but *is it as good* — measured across quality, creativity, and player satisfaction ([[Alecx Overview]]).
- Human-made content is generally seen as carrying more originality and emotional depth, tied to lived experience and audience understanding.
