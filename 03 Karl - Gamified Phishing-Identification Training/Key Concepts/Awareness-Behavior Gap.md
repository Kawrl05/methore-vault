# Awareness-Behavior Gap

## Definition
The finding that having cybersecurity *awareness* (knowing what a phishing email looks like, in theory) does not reliably predict safer *behavior* (actually avoiding malicious links).

## Key Details
- [[Rosano et al 2026]] measured high average awareness — 5.43 out of 7 — among 403 Filipino college students, but found **no significant relationship** between awareness and safe link-handling behavior (p = 0.098).
- This directly undercuts the standard assumption behind most cybersecurity education programs: that more knowledge content leads to safer behavior.
- It's the core motivating problem behind [[Karl Overview]] — if awareness alone doesn't change behavior, the training *method* (e.g., gamified simulation vs. seminar) may matter more than the content itself.
