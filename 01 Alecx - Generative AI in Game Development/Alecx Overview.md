# Alecx — Evaluating Generative AI in Creating Game Content

## Research Question
How does generative AI-created content compare with human-created content in terms of quality, creativity, and player satisfaction?

## Background
Generative AI can produce game content — dialogue and other creative assets — quickly, freeing developers to focus on other design work. Adoption is accelerating: [[Lambe 2025|Steam disclosure data]] shows roughly 7% of games released on the platform now disclose some AI use in content creation, up from about 1% the year before. Even so, developer sentiment is mixed, with both optimism and skepticism about AI's role in creative workflows ([[Boucher et al 2024]]).

The open question is whether AI output can match the originality and emotional depth that comes from human creators drawing on personal experience and audience understanding. See [[AI-Generated Game Content]] for a closer look at this comparison.

## Objectives
1. Compare the perceived quality and creativity of AI-generated vs. human-created content.
2. Measure player satisfaction across both content types.
3. Identify the advantages and disadvantages of generative AI as a game-development tool.
