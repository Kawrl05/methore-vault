# Player Perception of AI-Generated Content

## Definition
How players judge game content once they know (or believe) it was made by AI — as distinct from how "good" the content objectively is.

## Key Details
- In [[Mahsa Cooper 2026]], players could **not** reliably distinguish AI-designed levels from human-designed ones by playing them.
- Despite that, their *beliefs* about origin strongly shaped their ratings: content believed human-made was rated more fun and aesthetically pleasing; content believed AI-generated was rated more frustrating, overly challenging, and less original.
- This is a **belief-driven bias**, not a quality-driven one — meaning developer transparency about AI use could itself affect player reception, independent of actual content quality.
